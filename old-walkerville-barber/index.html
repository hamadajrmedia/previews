Chairs, towels and the old photos on the wall

Grandfather’s chairs, Tony’s shop

Tony took over his grandfather’s shop chairs with one goal: keep the old-school straight razor shave alive. It’s a craft that’s easy to lose, and he wasn’t willing to let it go.

Step inside and you’ll see how seriously he takes the past. Old photos cover the walls, and customers love looking through them while they wait. The hot towel shave is done the slow, careful way, and the classic cuts are the kind a barber can be proud of.

There’s nothing flashy here, and that’s deliberate. It’s a barbershop that does the basics properly, in the same chairs that started it all.
