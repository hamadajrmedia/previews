Fresh out the chair

From the team bench to the chair

Jay started out cutting hair for his basketball team. Nobody was paying for a fancy shop. Teammates sat down, he picked up the clippers, and word got around.

Cutting for the team showed him what a good cut does for a person. It's confidence before the game, before the night out, before Monday. So he built it into a studio, and what he cared about most was the culture.

That's why Blendz Studio sounds the way it does. The music is always on, the designs are crispy, and the hours run late so you can get fresh after work or school. Come in for the cut and stay for the vibe.
