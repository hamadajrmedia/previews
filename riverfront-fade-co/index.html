Inside the shop

Everyone's a regular here

Marcus learned to cut hair in his mom's basement, working on his friends' heads one Saturday at a time. Those sessions had good conversation and no one watching the clock, and he never forgot how that felt.

Riverfront Fade Co. is that basement, grown up. Marcus built a shop where everyone feels like a regular, even on their first visit. You get a clean fade, a chair that isn't rushed, and someone who's actually listening.

Whether you're in for a skin fade, a beard trim, a hot towel shave or a kid's first proper cut, the idea is the same. Come in, sit down, and leave looking sharp.
